## Abstract

XTT, which is a cubical reconstruction of observational type theory designed to
combine extensional equality principles with a computational interpretation, is
known to satisfy closed Boolean canonicity. A normalization theorem for
univalent Cartesian cubical type theory yields decidability of judgmental
equality. Still, it remains unclear whether the result can be extended to XTT.
We make partial progress on this question by introducing XTT-minus, a
universe-free cubical fragment of XTT with booleans, dependent products and
sums, proof-irrelevant dependent paths, regular coercion, and homogeneous
composition over arbitrary finite systems. In this talk, we prove that
judgmental conversion for XTT-minus is decidable for open terms in arbitrary
finite dimension and face contexts. A complete Agda formalization is in
progress.

(Jilin Wang is a PhD student at Zhejiang University.)
